Website builder for your own server

The website builder you set up once and hand to the people who run the business.

Talea comes with finished designs, editing right on the page and bookings, forms and a newsletter built in. One installation can run the websites of all your locations or stores, on your own server.

Install Talea View the looks

One installation for every location

A business with several locations, stores or brands runs all their websites from one installation. Each location has its own address, pages, opening hours and people, who can be limited to their own site.

  • One look for all. Publish a new design on the main site and every location takes it over. The locations cannot change it on their own.
  • One list for enquiries and bookings. Enquiries and bookings from every location arrive in one list, which you can narrow down to a single site.
  • Bookings with confirmation. Customers pick a time online and get a confirmation by e-mail with a link to cancel. For services that need it, you confirm each request first.
  • Shared photos and files. What you upload once is available on every site.

Start from a finished design

Each of the 14 looks sets colours, fonts, header and footer. You try a look on your own pages before you apply it, and changing the brand colour later restyles the whole site.

Will they really edit it on their own?

They click a headline on the page and type over it, and drag a photo in from the desktop. Phone and tablet are one click away. The design lives in shared styles, so a new text leaves the layout alone.

You decide who may publish and who only writes drafts. Every published page keeps its last 20 versions, and anything deleted waits in the trash for 30 days.

What people ask before they install it

Can my client edit it without calling me?+

Yes, on the page itself. You give each person exactly the rights they need, from writing drafts to publishing.

Do I need plugins for a contact form or bookings?+

No. Forms, bookings and a newsletter are built in.

Who keeps it updated?+

Whoever installed it pulls a new image. There are no plugins with updates of their own.

Can I take everything with me?+

Yes. Talea exports the whole site as an open archive that another Talea installation, or your own code, can read.

Can I run several locations or stores?+

Yes. One installation runs a website for each location, with one shared look, one media library and one list for enquiries and bookings. Each location keeps its own address, pages and people.

Can I sell online with it?+

You can show products with prices and variants and collect order enquiries in a basket. Checkout and payments are not built in.

Why self-host at all?+

The business keeps its site, its customer data and its costs in its own hands.

Why Talea is a fork of Kaleta

Christian started Talea in October 2026 from Kaleta 3.7.0, a CMS for business websites by Miroslav Kaleta and contributors. The page builder, the design system, bookings, forms, the newsletter, collections and the AI connection came with Kaleta.

Kaleta is made for business websites on ordinary PHP hosting, and it leaves some things out on purpose: finished designs, a font library, AI inside the admin, member areas and A/B tests. Those decisions suit Kaleta and its users. A website builder that an owner runs alone needs exactly these things, so Talea became a project of its own. It keeps Kaleta’s history, and security fixes from Kaleta are ported first.

For the business this means the websites of several locations from one installation, with one look and one list for enquiries and bookings, and the other differences in the table below.

Changes in Kaleta after the fork are read and rebuilt in Talea, so far through Kaleta 3.11.0. If you want ordinary PHP hosting with updates from the admin, Kaleta is the better fit.

Three commands on a server with Docker

Talea runs as one container next to a database. After the commands, the setup in the browser asks for the site name and the first administrator.

Terminal 
curl -O https://raw.githubusercontent.com/czepter/taleacms/main/docker-compose.yaml
echo "TALEA_DB_PASSWORD=$(openssl rand -hex 16)" > .env
docker compose up -d
# then open http://localhost:8080
For the technical folks+
  • Plain PHP 8.5 without a framework or build step, licensed GPL-2.0-or-later.
  • MySQL 8, MariaDB 11.4+, PostgreSQL 18 or SQLite.
  • Signed images for amd64 and arm64. Migrations run before the site starts.
  • Several sites in one installation with TALEA_MULTISITE=1, each answering to its own domain.
  • An MCP server with 173 tools, covering what the editor can do.
  • No third-party scripts or fonts on the published site.
