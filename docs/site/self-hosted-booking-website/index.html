Talea / Self-hosted booking website

A self-hosted booking website for a small business

Talea has bookings built in, so a small business can offer appointments on a website that runs on its own server. Visitors pick a service, a person or anyone, a day and a free time, and receive a confirmation by e-mail with a link to cancel. Payments and deposits are not included.

Last checked 31 October 2026

How a booking works for the visitor

  1. The visitor picks a service.
  2. The visitor picks a person, or chooses anyone.
  3. The visitor picks a day and one of the free times.
  4. The visitor leaves a name, an e-mail address and a phone number.
  5. A confirmation arrives by e-mail with a link to cancel the booking.

What the business controls

  • Services and people. You define what can be booked and who offers it.
  • Confirmation by the provider. A service can require the provider to confirm first. Until then the booking has the status “waiting for confirmation”.
  • Several locations. Each location has its own bookings and opening hours, and you can list bookings across all sites or narrow the list to one.
  • The data. Bookings live in the database of your own installation, next to the rest of the site. Backups go to FTPS or S3 on a schedule.

Bookings as part of a whole website

The booking pages sit on the same site as everything else. Enquiry forms, a newsletter, news and members-only pages are built in, and the owner edits texts right on the page. This suits a salon, a practice, a workshop or a tutor who wants one website instead of a site plus a separate booking tool.

Talea runs as one container next to a database. The install section on the home page shows the three commands, and the setup in the browser asks for the site name and the first administrator.

What Talea's bookings do not do

  • No payments and no deposits. A booking reserves a time and nothing is charged.
  • No hosted option. Talea runs on a server of your own with Docker, and someone has to run that server.
  • No online checkout elsewhere on the site either. Products can be shown, and order enquiries are collected in a basket.

When to choose something else

  • Customers must pay or leave a deposit when they book. Choose a tool that takes payments.
  • Nobody can run a server. A hosted builder with booking, such as Wix, leaves the upkeep to the vendor.
  • You need only a booking widget on an existing site. A single-purpose scheduling product may be enough.

Questions people ask

Can customers pay when they book?+

No. Talea's bookings have no payments or deposits. The booking reserves a time and the business settles payment in its usual way.

Do customers get a confirmation?+

Yes. Visitors receive a confirmation by e-mail after they book, and it contains a link to cancel.

Can I approve each booking myself?+

Yes, for services that need it. A service can require the provider's confirmation first, and the booking then has the status waiting for confirmation.

Do I need a plugin for bookings?+

No. Bookings are part of Talea, together with forms and a newsletter, so there is no plugin to choose or to update.

Can customers choose a person?+

Yes. Visitors pick a person, or anyone, and then a day and a free time.
