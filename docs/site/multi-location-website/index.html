A website for multiple locations from one installation

A business with several locations can run one website per location from a single Talea installation. Each location has its own domain, pages, opening hours, bookings and people, while the look, the media library and the list of enquiries and bookings are shared. This guide explains how to plan it and what it cannot do.

Last checked 31 October 2026

Plan the locations first

Give every location what is true only there: its address, opening hours, the people who work there, the services it offers and its own photos. Pages that repeat the same text with a different city name tell a visitor very little.

Then decide how the sites relate. In Talea each location's site answers to its own domain, and the main site can link to all of them. Whether separate domains suit you depends on how customers find you and how separate the brands are.

One installation, one site per location

Several sites are switched on with one setting, TALEA_MULTISITE=1, when Talea is installed. After that you add a site for each location. What is shared and what is separate:

Shared by all locationsSeparate for each location
One look. The main site hands it down when you publish, and the locations cannot change it.Its own domain
One media library. What you upload once is available on every site.Its own pages and opening hours
One list of enquiries and bookings, which you can narrow down to a single site.Its own bookings and people. People can be limited to their own site.

How a new design reaches every location

You publish the look on the main site and every location takes it over. A location manager can edit the pages of their site but cannot restyle it. This keeps the brand consistent without anyone copying settings from site to site.

Bookings and enquiries across locations

Customers book on the site of the location they choose: they pick a service, a person or anyone, a day and a free time, and receive a confirmation by e-mail with a cancel link. The business can list enquiries and bookings from every location in one place, or narrow the list to one site.

Who looks after what

One person or agency runs the installation: the server, the backups to FTPS or S3, and updates. Because all locations are one installation, one new container image updates every site at once. Location managers work inside their own site and do not touch the server.

Each location keeps its own opening hours, so a visitor sees the hours of the place they are looking at and not those of the whole company.

Compared with WordPress Multisite

WordPress describes Multisite as a feature that runs several sites from one installation, with its own tables for each site and a shared user table. Talea has the same aim and adds a shared look and media library, with people who can be limited to a site.

WordPress offers a very large choice of plugins and themes for each site, which Talea does not. Choose by what the sites need.

When to choose something else

  • Locations need different designs. Talea shares one look that the locations cannot change, so separate installations suit that case.
  • Each location needs online checkout. Talea has no checkout and no payments.
  • Your host cannot run Docker, which Talea needs.
  • You want one domain with every location under it. Talea gives each site its own domain.

Questions people ask

Should each location have its own domain?+

In Talea each location's site answers to its own domain. Whether separate domains suit your business depends on how customers find you and how separate the brands are. The main site can link to every location.

Can location managers edit only their own site?+

Yes. People can be limited to their own site, so a manager edits the pages, hours and bookings of one location.

Can I see bookings from all locations in one list?+

Yes. Enquiries and bookings from every location arrive in one list, which you can narrow down to a single site.

Can a location change the design?+

No. The main site hands the look down when you publish, and the locations cannot change it on their own.

Does each location need its own server?+

No. One installation runs all the sites. You switch this on with the setting TALEA_MULTISITE=1 when Talea is installed.
