## 3. Pages worth writing

Talea facts for claims come from the repo (CLAUDE.md), not from this research; do not state anything about rivals beyond the verified rows above.

1. **"Wix alternative you can host yourself" / "Squarespace alternative self-hosted"**. Target: wix alternative self-hosted, squarespace alternative self-hosted. Intent: leave/replace. Angle: state plainly what Wix does not allow (site only on Wix servers; templates not downloadable) and what Squarespace exports (WordPress XML, limited page types, no styles); then what moving to Talea means, including the existing WordPress (WXR) import path. No price claims about rivals beyond the verified plan tables.
2. **"WordPress alternative for small business"**. Target: wordpress alternative (for small business / self-hosted). Intent: compare. Angle: honest table on what is built in vs plugin-dependent (booking, forms, newsletter, members-only, several sites), who updates, and a "when to stay on WordPress" section. WordPress facts: GPLv2+, PHP 8.3+, Multisite exists, auto-updates limited for plugins.
3. **"Best self-hosted CMS for a small business site"**. Target: best self-hosted cms, open source website builder. Intent: shortlist. Angle: short comparison of WordPress, Joomla, Ghost, Grav, Kirby, Talea with requirements and licence rows from section 2 (PHP versions, DB or not, licence, price), marking unverified cells as "not checked". Differentiator: no ranking by affiliate pay, no "winner for everyone".
4. **"Website for several locations from one installation"**. Target: website for multiple locations, multi-location business website. Intent: guidance. Angle: agency content covers SEO (page per location, no thin city pages, one domain vs many); add the tooling answer: separate sites sharing one install and design, with per-location content. Link to the one-domain-vs-several decision.
5. **"Self-hosted booking website with confirmation"**. Target: self-hosted booking website small business. Intent: find tool. Angle: results are hosted widgets or marketplace scripts; Talea offers bookings with confirmation built in plus forms, in a full site; state the limit: no checkout/payments.
6. **"Best website builder / CMS for small business 2027"** (index/guide hub). Target: best cms for small business 2027, best website builder for small business 2027. Intent: decide. Angle: decision guide by situation (want no upkeep -> hosted builder; want a store -> a shop platform; want own site, no monthly platform fee, finished designs -> Talea). Honest about the cost of self-hosting (server, backups, updates). Date-stamp 2026/2027; competitive, so treat as a hub that links to pages 1-5.
7. **FAQ page "Leaving a website builder"**. Target: long-tail 1-4 (export Wix/Squarespace, is Wix self-hosted). Intent: informational. Angle: quote verified vendor limits with dates and links to vendor support pages.
8. **FAQ entries on every page**: who updates it, what it costs to run, what is not included (checkout/payments), how to move in/out, MCP/AI-assistant connection explained in one sentence.
